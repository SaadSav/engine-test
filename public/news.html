Game Recap October 28, 2025

Thunderbolts Secure Overtime Victory in Season Opener

The Thunderbolts kicked off their season with an exciting 4-3 overtime win against the visiting Ice Hawks. Captain Sarah Johnson scored the game-winning goal just 2 minutes into the extra period, sending the home crowd into a frenzy.

The match showcased exceptional goaltending from both sides, with Thunderbolts' netminder making 35 saves throughout the game. The team displayed remarkable resilience, coming back from a 2-0 deficit in the second period.

Player News October 27, 2025

Rising Star Mike Chen Signs Three-Year Contract Extension

In a major announcement today, the Thunderbolts confirmed that forward Mike Chen has signed a three-year contract extension. The 24-year-old had an impressive rookie season, recording 28 goals and 35 assists in 72 games.

"Mike has proven himself to be a cornerstone of our team's future," said General Manager David Thompson. "His work ethic, skill level, and leadership qualities make him invaluable to our organization."

Team Update October 25, 2025

Thunderbolts Announce New Practice Facility Opening

The Thunderbolts organization unveiled plans for a state-of-the-art practice facility set to open in December. The 50,000 square-foot complex will feature two regulation-size ice rinks, a strength and conditioning center, and advanced video analysis rooms.

The new facility represents a $25 million investment in the team's infrastructure and will also serve as a community resource, offering youth hockey programs and public skating sessions.
